# Agent A report: Discovery Mode mechanics, surfaces, eligibility, scale (as of 2026-10-02)
[S] Spotify statement; [3P] third party; [INF] inference. All via search renderings (direct fetch blocked).

## 1. Mechanics
- Launch 2 Nov 2020 [S]: rights holders "agree to be paid a promotional recording royalty rate for streams in personalized listening sessions where we provided this service"; "won't require any upfront budget". [1]
- Current terms [S]: "A 30% commission is applied to recording royalties generated from all streams of selected songs and their related track URIs in Discovery Mode contexts. All other streams remain commission-free." Deducted from future statements. [2]
- Recording royalties only; publishing untouched [INF].
- Spotify keeps the 30%: 20-F FY2024/FY2025: "Cost of revenue reflects discounts provided by certain rights holders in return for promotional activities in connection with marketplace programs" [3,4]. Q2-25 letter: Premium GM gains from "revenue growth outpacing music royalty costs net of certain marketplace programs" [5]; Q4-25 deck (10 Feb 2026, GM 33.1%): "music costs net of marketplace programs" [6].
- Investor Day 8 Jun 2022: Marketplace gross-profit contribution "<EUR20m" (2018) to ">EUR160m" (2021); "These marketplace businesses have been the primary factor in growing our music gross margins" [7]. Investor Day 21 May 2026: Marketplace gross profit ~4x 2021 [3P recap, 31].
- Royalties otherwise paid by streamshare [8]. TuneCore passes a 20% commission to its artists [3P, 9].

## 2. Surfaces and dates
- 2 Nov 2020: Radio and Autoplay. [1]
- Q2-21 letter: test with "major labels, independent labels, and independent artist distributors"; broader rollout later 2021. [10]
- 8 Mar 2023 Stream On: self-serve in S4A via participating distributors; "thousands of independently distributed artists and labels now have access". [11,12]
- Daily Mix added 3 Jan 2024, then Artist, Genre, Decade, Mood Mixes. [13,14]
- Current contexts: "Spotify Radio, Autoplay, and Spotify Mixes (Daily, Artist, Mood, Decade, and Genre Mixes)". Smart Shuffle, DW, RR, AI DJ NOT included. [15]
- Sept 2025 new T&Cs (song-level opt-in); non-re-accepted campaigns cancelled 30 Apr 2026; commission unchanged 30%. [16]

## 3. Eligibility / participation
- Artist team self-serve: >=3 eligible songs, >=25,000 monthly listeners, eligible country (90+ markets); song: released >=30 days, >=20 streams in DM contexts in last 28 days, distributed by a participating licensor. Labels via partnerships team. [2,17,18]
- Participating S4A licensors: Amuse, CD Baby, CmdShft, DistroKid, EmuBands, EVEARA, Feiyr, Horus, LANDR, Lanzadera, MyMusic, OFFstep, Rebeat, recordJet, Repost, Soundrop, Stem, UnitedMasters, Venice [17]. Believe/TuneCore in 2021 test [19]; Believe CEO (~Sept 2025): moves "hundreds of thousands of tracks every month" in/out of DM, ">98%" positive returns [20].
- Majors: Q2-21 letter says "major labels" in the test, none named [10]; one unnamed major tested (MBW 2021) [21]; UMG/WMG criticised; IMPALA asked CMA to investigate (Jan 2022) [22]. Substack claims Sony bars DM (unverified) [23]. 2025 deals (UMG 26 Jan, WMG 6 Feb, Sony 18 Sep, Merlin 30 Sep) do not mention DM in renderings seen [24-27].

## 4. Scale statistics
- No Spotify disclosure of tracks/artists enrolled, share of catalog, DM share of context streams, or DM stream totals.
- Efficacy claims [S]: Q2-21 "over 40% more listeners"; current page "+186% monthly playlist adds and +181% monthly saves", "+106% monthly listeners"; "16 billion artist discoveries every month", "a third" via algorithmic contexts; "In 2024, 35% of all discoveries ... in algorithmic contexts". [10,18,34]
- Congress letters 2021/2022; no "small share of listening" phrase found. [32-36]
- Capolongo class action 4 Nov 2025 (SDNY); arbitration compelled 30 Apr 2026. [37,38]

## 5. Share of listening
- F-1 2018: ~31% programmed. "Made to Be Found" Jan 2022: >half of new-artist discoveries in programmed playlists, "over a quarter" from Mixes, Radio, Autoplay (discoveries). [39,40]

## 6. Caps / guardrails
- "increases the likelihood - but does not guarantee"; promotion reduced when skip rates high; no numeric cap. Eligibility floor (>=20 DM-context streams / 28 days). [1,2,18,32]
