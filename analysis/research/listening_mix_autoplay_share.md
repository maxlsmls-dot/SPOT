# Agent B report: lean-back / algorithmic share of streams (as of 2026-10-02)

Bottom line: Spotify has never disclosed the share of streams in Radio, Autoplay, Mixes or Smart Shuffle.

## Spotify statements
- F-1 Feb 2018: "We now program approximately 31% of all listening on Spotify across these and other playlists, compared to less than 20% two years ago." Scope: all listening; Spotify-programmed playlists = editorial + algorithmic. [1]
- Newsroom 2 Nov 2020: "A third of discoveries on Spotify happen via personalized recommendations in algorithmic context, driving 16 billion artist discoveries every month." Scope: discoveries. [2]
- Q1 2021 letter: DM = "promotional recording royalty rate for streams in personalized listening sessions"; early results "30% increase in streams" for opted-in content. [3]
- Q2 2021 letter: "over 40% more listeners on average"; "44% of those listeners had never listened to the artist before". [4]
- S4A "Made to Be Found" 26 Jan 2022: streams bucketed "Made by Editors", "Made for You" (personalized playlists, autoplay, radio), "Made by You"; "the majority of streams" come from active sessions; no %. "More than half of new artist discoveries on Spotify happen in programmed playlists - with over a quarter coming from Spotify Mixes, Radio, and Autoplay"; "33% of new artist discoveries come from personalised streams". [7][8]
- Investor Day 8 Jun 2022: "One third of all new artist discoveries happen on personalized, algorithmic playlists"; discoveries 10bn/month (2018) -> 22bn/month (2022). [9]
- Newsroom 22 Jan 2025: "nearly 2 billion music discoveries every 24 hours". [10]
- Marketing-blog claim "Radio and Autoplay > 25% of all streams (Loud & Clear 2024)" is a conflation of the Jan-2022 discoveries stat. Not real.

## Third-party
- Your Music Marketing (Y2M), Feb 2024 via Music Ally: 25.3bn streams, several hundred artists, 2015-early 2024. 18.7% of streams from "algorithmic playlists and mixes" (radio, Daily Mix, Discover Weekly, Release Radar, On Repeat, Repeat Rewind); "just over 21%" in the last 12 months. Emerging artists 59.9% programmed listens (mostly user playlists); top artists 64.2% active. Autoplay treatment unspecified. [11]
- Academic: Anderson et al. WWW 2020 (Spotify Research) organic vs programmed; Bello & Garcia 2021 cite "according to Spotify, up to one-fifth of their streams can be attributed to algorithmic recommendations". [12][13]
- MIDiA: qualitative, "direction of travel is towards more passive listening". [14][15]
- Luminate / GS / MS / JPM: nothing found. "Luminate: algorithmic = 38%" untraceable, treat as spurious.

## S4A source-of-streams taxonomy and DM scope
- Active = profile & catalog; listener's own playlists & library; queue. Programmed = editorial; "Personalized playlists, autoplay, and mixes" (DW, RR, Radio, Autoplay, Daily Mix, daylist, DJ); other listeners' playlists. [16]
- DM contexts: Spotify Radio, Autoplay, Spotify Mixes (Daily Mix from 3 Jan 2024; Artist Mix from 1 Mar 2024; then decade/mood/genre mixes). Smart Shuffle, DJ, DW, RR, editorial are NOT DM contexts. Song eligibility: >=20 streams in DM contexts in last 28 days. [17][18][19]
- Podcasts/audiobooks/spoken word not supported by DM -> denominator = music streams. [17]

## Trend
- DJ: launched Feb 2023; "25% of their listening time with DJ" on days used (Mar 2023); "used by 94 million subscribers" (Q1 2026 call); "nearly 100 million Premium users" since launch (cumulative). [20][21][22][23]
- Smart Shuffle: Premium, Mar 2023, one recommended song per three tracks; Nov 2025 "Fewer Repeats" default. No usage numbers. [24][25]
- Autoplay: on by default (third-party guides). [26]
- Discoveries/month 10bn (2018) -> 16bn (2020) -> 22bn (2022) -> ~60bn (2025).

## Agent's inference
DM-eligible contexts (Radio+Autoplay+Mixes) plausibly ~10-20% of music streams, likely rising; no disclosed point estimate.
