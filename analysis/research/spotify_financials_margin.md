## Quarterly
- Q1'25 rev 4,190, GM 31.6%. Q2'25 4,190, GM 31.5%, ARPU 4.57. Q3'25 4,270, GM 31.6%, Premium GM 33.2%, Ad GM 18.4%, subs 281, MAU 713, ARPU 4.53.
- Q4'25 (10 Feb 26): rev 4,530 (+7% / +13% cc), GM 33.1%, OI 701, subs 290, MAU 751, ARPU 4.70. Q1'26 guide: rev 4,500, GM 32.8%.
- Q1'26 (28 Apr 26): rev 4,530 (+8%), Ad 385 (-5%), GM 33.0%, Premium GM 34.8%, Ad GM 13%, OI 715, MAU 761, subs 293. Q2 guide 4,800, GM 33.1%.
- Q2'26 (Aug 26): rev 4,780 (+14%), Premium 4,330 (+15%), Ad 446 (+1%), GM 33.4%, Premium GM 34.9%, Ad GM 19.1%, MAU 777, subs 300, ARPU 4.89 (+7.4% cc). Q3'26 guide: rev ~5,000, GM 32.9%, OI ~670.
- Investor Day 21 May 26: 2030 targets GM 35-40%, OM >20%, mid-teens rev CAGR; "about a third of the gross margin expansion came from the music business"; music and non-music both >30% GM.

## Royalty structure
- 20-F: CoR "consists predominantly of royalty and distribution costs ... also reflects discounts provided by certain rights holders in return for promotional activities in connection with marketplace programs". FY25 Premium CoR rise driven by "increased rates for certain licensors ... partially offset by benefits from certain marketplace programs"; royalty costs +765 y/y.
- Majors+Merlin share of streams: ~75% (2022), 74% (2023), 71% (2024), 72% (2025).
- Recording/publishing split not disclosed. MIDiA (2024 via Variety 2025): per stream 30% DSP / 56% recording / 14% publishing.
- CRB Phonorecords IV headline % of revenue: 15.1% (2023) ... 15.35% (2027).
- Bundle reclassification Mar 2024: Billboard est. ~$150m less US mechanicals in first 12 months; Spotify 6-K: extra royalties if MLC won ~EUR46m for 1 Mar-30 Jun 2024 (~EUR140m annualised). MLC suit dismissed 29 Jan 25; amended complaint 1 Oct 25. Direct publishing deals 2025 partly reverse savings.

## Management commentary
- Investor Day 8 Jun 2022: Marketplace GP contribution ">EUR160m" in 2021, 8x 2018; Discovery Mode "98% customer retention", "over 50 labels and distributors", revenue +224% y/y; marketplace "the primary factor in growing music gross margins"; music GM target 30-35%; 10-yr: $100bn rev, 40% GM, 20% OM.
- Q2'24 call (Ben Kung): GM 29.2%, "outperformance was driven primarily by music content cost favorability and marketplace."
- FY24 20-F: Premium GM up "due primarily to revenue growth outpacing music royalty costs and audiobook licensing costs, and benefits from certain marketplace programs, along with a reduction in streaming delivery costs"; Ad: "growth in benefits from certain marketplace programs".
- FY25 20-F: "revenue growth outpacing music royalty costs net of certain marketplace programs and audiobook licensing costs ... partially offset by ... Spotify Partner Program".
- Q1'26/Q2'26: Premium GM "driven by revenue growth outpacing music costs net of marketplace programs, audiobooks costs and video podcast costs".
- Q4'25 call (Luiga): GM expansion "from several building blocks, including in music, the marketplace and the ads monetization".
- No EUR marketplace disclosure after 2022 Investor Day found (Investor Day 2026 recap: ~4x 2021).

## Third-party DM estimates
- MIDiA / Mulligan (2025): "When a label uses Discovery Mode, Spotify gets an extra 17% of gross margin" on those streams (~30% x 56%).
- Morgan Stanley 2026 initiation (OW, PT $630): GM mid-30s by 2027 via price/mix; Bernstein (PT $625): "content cost efficiencies". Neither quantifies DM. No GS/JPM/MBW EUR or bps estimate found.

## Streams / royalties shares
- Loud & Clear: 2023 ~$9bn paid, independents ~$4.5bn ~half; 2024 >$10bn, independents >$5bn ~50%; 2025 >$11bn, independents ~50%. DM share never disclosed.
- Music Ally (Feb 2023): DIY artists ~a quarter of Spotify music streams (2022).
