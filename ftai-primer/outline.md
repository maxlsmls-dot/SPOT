# Outline — FTAI Aviation deep primer

Section numbers run continuously across Parts. Each Part is one markdown file in parts/. The Spine
is written last and placed first. Parts IV to XI open by naming the translation-table rows (Part III
§24) they answer; that is the organizing device, and it is enforced at validation.

## Layers
- (I) First principles: Parts I and II.
- (II) The context shock and the translation table: Part III.
- (III–V) The segments: Parts IV, V, VI (the market around FTAI); VII, VIII, IX (FTAI's businesses).
- (VI) Markets and money: Parts X and XI.
- (VII) Reference: Part XII.
The margin pool appears three times: Spine §3, Part I §11 (orientation), Part XI §86 (full).
