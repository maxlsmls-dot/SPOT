# Term registry — FTAI Aviation deep primer

Built 2026-10-03 from outline-skeleton.md, chain-map.md, orchestrator-notes.md and all eleven dossiers
(D1–D11, "Terms introduced" sections plus a skim of each body for load-bearing terms and acronyms used
but not listed). Definitions are the dossiers' own, tightened; the dossier and section are cited after
each. Nothing here is a view.

## How to read the registry

- Rows sit under the **Part that owns the full treatment** (per outline-skeleton.md), sorted
  alphabetically within the Part. Owner Parts define the term in a definition box and carry the
  mechanics, numbers and worked examples.
- **First use** is the earliest Part in reading order (I, II, III, IV, V, VI, VII, VIII, IX, X, XI, XII;
  Spine excluded) where a writer will inevitably need the term. When first use differs from the owner,
  the first-use Part adds a **short definition box** (one or two sentences and a cross-reference to the
  owner's section) and the owner carries the full treatment. The **Box** column says which.
- **Spine** marks the 30 to 40 terms the Spine cannot avoid (list in section 4). The Spine defines
  exactly those and no more.
- Section numbers (§) in citations refer to the dossier's own numbering. Cross-references to primer
  sections use outline.md numbers.
- Where a term is defined differently by two dossiers, the row says "see C-n" and section 3 quotes both.
- Section 1b, after the tables, lists per Part the terms that Part must box although it does not own
  them (the first-use index). Section 1c lists terms the skeleton assigns that no dossier defines.
