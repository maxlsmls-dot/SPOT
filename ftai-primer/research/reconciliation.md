# Reconciliation pass — FTAI Aviation deep primer

Written 2026-10-03. This pass read, in full: the reconciliation skill and its method reference; the chain map; the orchestrator notes; the outline skeleton; and dossiers D1 through D11 (about 99,000 words). It read nothing else. No web search or fetch was used.

Posture. This document surfaces seams between the dossiers. It does not resolve any of them. Where two sources sit at different levels (SEC filing, company press release, OEM or peer filing, appraiser publication, trade press, consultancy or market-research blog, call-coverage summary, investor blog), that level is stated as a fact about provenance. It is never converted into a verdict about which figure is right. Every number below is quoted from a dossier or from the orchestrator notes; nothing is re-sourced.
